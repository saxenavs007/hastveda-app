Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

HastVeda ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the HastVeda mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Palm Images: Photos of your palm captured for AI analysis. These are processed to generate your reading and are not permanently stored without your consent.
  • Account Information: Email address and display name if you create an account or sign in with Google.
  • Reading History: Your past palm readings and reports, stored securely in your account.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics data about how you use the app (features used, session duration) to improve the experience.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for technical support purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the palm reading service
  • To generate personalized readings and reports
  • To maintain your reading history and account
  • To send notifications about your readings (with your permission)
  • To improve AI accuracy and app performance
  • To respond to support requests

3. AI Processing

Palm images are sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policies. We do not sell your palm images or reading data to third parties. Images are transmitted securely over encrypted connections.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data and reading history for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@hastveda.app.

6. Third-Party Services

HastVeda uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini AI — Palm image analysis
  • Supabase — Database and authentication
  • Mixpanel — Anonymous usage analytics
  • Google Sign-In — Optional authentication

7. Children's Privacy

HastVeda is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@hastveda.app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@hastveda.app.
